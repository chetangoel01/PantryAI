### App Store Description

**Transform your kitchen with PantryAI - the intelligent pantry management app that makes meal planning effortless!**

📱 **Smart Grocery Scanning**
• Scan receipts and grocery items with advanced OCR technology
• Automatically identify products, quantities, and expiration dates
• No manual data entry required - just point and scan!

🏠 **Smart Pantry Management**
• Keep track of all your food items in one place
• Get expiration date alerts to reduce food waste
• Organize items by categories and locations
• Track quantities and set reorder reminders

👨‍🍳 **AI-Powered Recipe Recommendations**
• Get personalized recipe suggestions based on your pantry
• Discover new dishes you can make with ingredients you have
• Save your favorite recipes for quick access
• Filter recipes by dietary preferences and cooking time

📊 **Smart Shopping Lists**
• Generate shopping lists based on your pantry and meal plans
• Never forget essential ingredients again
• Share lists with family members
• Track your grocery spending

🔒 **Privacy-First Design**
• All OCR processing happens on your device
• Your data stays private and secure
• No ads or data sharing with third parties
